# C4 - Tunnel Delivery Verification

## Test 1: Delivery to h2

Command:

```bash
h1 python3 send.py 10.83.0.2 "test h2" --tunnel 2 --hops 5
```

Result:
- Packet successfully transmitted from h1.
- Destination tunnel ID: 2.
- Initial hop-limit: 5.
- Packet captured at h2 using tcpdump.
- Remaining hop-limit at h2: 3.
- Payload: `test h2`.

## Test 2: Delivery to h3

Command:

```bash
h1 python3 send.py 10.83.0.3 "test h3" --tunnel 3 --hops 5
```

Result:
- Packet successfully transmitted from h1.
- Destination tunnel ID: 3.
- Initial hop-limit: 5.
- Packet captured at h3 using tcpdump.
- Remaining hop-limit at h3: 3.
- Payload: `test h3`.



## Conclusion

Both tunnel delivery tests were successful. The packet captures confirmed that the custom tunnel header was correctly transmitted and parsed. The hop-limit decreased from 5 to 3 after passing through s1 and s2, demonstrating that each switch decremented the hop-limit once before forwarding the packet.
